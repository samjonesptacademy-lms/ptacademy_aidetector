Three things are different, and one of them changes how you read every answer:

  • Answers needing review come first, most serious at the top — with the specific sentences flagged.
  • Verdict is the primary signal; AI Score is secondary and the two can disagree. Where they differ, follow the Verdict.

Reports issued before 23 July 2026 used the old logic and are worth re-running.
